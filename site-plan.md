# Good News Corner — Website Blueprint

## Business Overview
- **Name:** Good News Corner
- **Type:** Charcuterie board business
- **Service Area:** DFW (Dallas/Fort Worth), Texas
- **Offerings:** Set menu boards/boxes + custom orders
- **Fulfillment:** Delivery, pickup, and catering/events

---

## Site Structure

### 1. HOME PAGE
**Purpose:** First impression — showcase the product and make it easy to order.

**Sections:**
- **Hero Banner**
  - Large, high-quality photo (recommend: Manna_Board.jpg or Harvest_Board.jpg — most visually striking)
  - Headline: "Handcrafted Charcuterie for Every Occasion"
  - Subheadline: "Serving the Dallas/Fort Worth area"
  - CTA button: "View Our Menu" / "Place an Order"

- **About Snippet**
  - 2-3 sentences about Good News Corner (the story, the mission)
  - Link to full About page

- **Featured Boards**
  - Grid or carousel showing 3-4 of the signature boards with names and starting prices
  - Each links to the full Menu page

- **How It Works**
  - 3 simple steps: Browse Menu → Place Order → Enjoy
  - Mention delivery, pickup, and catering options

- **Testimonials / Social Proof**
  - Customer reviews (pull from Google, Instagram, etc.)

- **Instagram Feed**
  - Embed or link to Instagram (great for a visual business like this)

- **Footer**
  - Contact info, service area, social links, copyright

---

---

### 3. CUSTOM ORDERS PAGE
**Purpose:** Capture leads for custom/event orders.

**Content:**
- Intro text: "Have something special in mind? We'd love to create a custom board tailored to your event, dietary needs, or theme."
- What they can customize: size, ingredients, dietary accommodations, themed boards (holidays, bridal, corporate)
- **Custom Order Form** (Wix Forms or a connected Google Form):
  - Name
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Event date
  - Event type (dropdown: Birthday, Wedding/Bridal, Corporate, Holiday, Other)
  - Estimated guest count
  - Budget range
  - Special requests / dietary needs
  - How did you hear about us?

---

### 4. CATERING & EVENTS PAGE
**Purpose:** Market to event planners, corporate clients, and celebrations.

**Content:**
- Types of events served: weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, corporate events, holidays, game day, girls' night, etc.
- What's included (setup, disposable or reusable boards, serving utensils, etc.)
- Photo gallery of event setups (if available — or use existing board photos for now)
- Minimum order / lead time info (e.g., "Please allow 48-72 hours for custom and catering orders")
- CTA: Link to Custom Order form

---

### 5. ABOUT PAGE
**Purpose:** Build trust and personal connection.

**Suggested Content Outline:**
- The story behind Good News Corner (why she started, what "Good News" means to her)
- Her passion for food and bringing people together
- Photo of the owner (if comfortable)
- What makes Good News Corner different:
  - Handcrafted with care
  - Locally sourced when possible
  - Branded personal touches (the stamped chocolates, etc.)
- Service area: DFW, Texas

---

### 6. CONTACT PAGE
**Purpose:** Make it easy to reach her.

**Content:**
- Contact form (Name, Email, Message)
- Email address
- Phone number (optional — or use a business texting number)
- Instagram / Facebook links
- Service area map or note: "Proudly serving Dallas, Fort Worth, and surrounding areas"
- Business hours / order lead times

---

### 7. FAQ PAGE (optional but recommended)
**Purpose:** Reduce repetitive inquiries.

**Suggested Questions:**
- How far in advance should I place my order?
- Do you deliver? What areas do you serve?
- Can you accommodate dietary restrictions (nut-free, gluten-free, etc.)?
- How should I store my board/box before serving?
- Do you offer tastings?
- What is your cancellation/refund policy?
- Do boards come with serving utensils?
- Can I reuse or return the wooden boards?

## Design Recommendations

### Template Style
- Look for Wix templates under **"Restaurant"** or **"Catering"** categories
- Choose something with large image areas, clean fonts, and warm tones
- Recommended Wix templates to search for: "Food", "Catering", or "Bakery" templates

### Color Palette (suggestions based on the brand aesthetic)
- **Primary:** Warm gold/honey (#C8973E) — matches the honey jars and warm tones in photos
- **Secondary:** Deep burgundy/wine (#6B2737) — complements the meats and grapes
- **Neutral:** Cream/off-white (#FAF5EF) — matches the marble backdrop in photos
- **Text:** Dark charcoal (#2C2C2C)

### Fonts
- **Headings:** A serif font like Playfair Display or Cormorant (elegant, artisan feel)
- **Body:** A clean sans-serif like Lato or Montserrat (easy to read)

### Photography Tips
- The existing photos are excellent — great lighting and composition
- For the website hero, the Harvest Board or Manna Board would be most impactful
- Consider adding lifestyle shots if possible (boards at a party, someone serving, etc.)
