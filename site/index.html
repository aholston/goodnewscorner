Our Story

"How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!" — Romans 10:15

At The Good News Corner, our name says it all — we're inspired by the greatest good news ever told, the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Every board and box we create is handcrafted with love, made to bring people together the way Jesus did — around a table. Intentional Gatherings with Intentional Ingredients.

Globally

When you choose us, you're helping us support a cause that we hold close to our hearts. We are committed to faith and community, which is why 10% of all proceeds are donated to Godlink.org, a non profit committed to building a better future for Zambian children by providing food, clean water, shelter, and the opportunity to an excellent education.

Serving the Dallas/Fort Worth community with love, intention, and a whole lot of flavor.
