Our Story

"Every good and perfect gift is from above." — James 1:17

At The Good News Corner, our name says it all — we're inspired by the greatest good news ever told, the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Every board we create is handcrafted with love, made to bring people together the way Jesus did — around a table. We pour purpose into every board because we believe breaking bread together is something worth celebrating.

Serving the Dallas/Fort Worth community with love, intention, and a whole lot of flavor.
